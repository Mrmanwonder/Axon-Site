Rules kept

  • Blue means locked or primary. Amber (with a dashed outline and words) means a page needs attention. No red anywhere.
  • The shutter is never blocked by quality checks; only Auto can refuse.
  • No decorative motion during capture. One short flash and the page sliding into the stack, after the shot.
  • Reduced motion removes the flash.
