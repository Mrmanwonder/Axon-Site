# AXON Production Constitution

# Chapter 1 — Vision

Axon exists to turn marked academic work into trustworthy, actionable feedback.

The product should reduce the distance between receiving a marked paper and
understanding what to improve. It does this by extracting the paper carefully,
preserving provenance, asking for review when uncertain, explaining mistakes,
and aggregating confirmed outcomes into useful insights.

Axon does **not** maintain an Academic Digital Twin, generate missions, or
operate a study-planning engine. Those concepts belong to an abandoned product
direction and are not part of the current architecture.

The product's defining promise is:

> Every important claim about a student's work should be traceable to evidence,
> explicitly uncertain, or withheld.
