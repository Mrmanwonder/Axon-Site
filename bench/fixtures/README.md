# Real capture fixtures

Photographs taken through the app's own viewfinder on a real phone, on a real
desk, of a real Cambridge paper — supplied after the first build of stage 0 got
the page badly wrong on all of them.

`viewfinder-a.jpg` and `viewfinder-b.jpg` are full screenshots: the live feed
with the detected quad drawn over it. They are the evidence that the detector
was latching onto the whole bright region of the desk rather than the page, and
the reason the corner brackets sat near the frame edges.

The `page-*.jpg` files are what the scanner actually stored.

These are worth more than any synthetic page for stage 0, because the failure
they show — a white page on a pale desk beside two bright folders — is the
normal case and is exactly what a brightness-based detector cannot see.

Not a golden set. That is twenty labelled papers for measuring *extraction*
accuracy; these measure whether capture finds the page at all.
