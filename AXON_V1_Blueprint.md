
# AXON V1 Product Blueprint

## Product Vision
AXON is not an AI study app.
AXON is the mentor every student wishes they had.

Core promise:
- Tell students exactly what to study.
- Explain why.
- Learn from every mistake.
- Reduce anxiety through clarity.

## Audience
- CAIE IGCSE, AS & A Level
- Ages 13–17
- Students targeting high academic performance.

## Design Philosophy
"Quiet Intelligence"

Principles:
- Calm before impressive.
- Minimal information.
- Large whitespace.
- Editorial typography.
- Motion has purpose.
- One primary task visible at a time.

## Core Pages
1. Landing
2. Authentication
3. Dashboard
4. Study Planner
5. Analytics
6. AI Mentor
7. Settings

## Dashboard
Questions answered:
- Where am I?
- What should I do today?
- Why?

Sections:
- Exam countdown
- Today's mission
- Continue studying
- Weakness insights
- Recent mistakes
- AI mentor

## Beta Features
- Authentication
- Planner
- Analytics
- AI Mentor
- Past paper launcher
- Progress tracking

## Remove From V1
- University search
- Voice
- OCR
- Leaderboards
- Social
- Cloud backup
- Offline sync
- Multi-model selection

## Brand
Premium, calm, confident, mentor-like.
